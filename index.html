Activity 6: Listen and Read

Comprehension

Diego's Weekend

My name is Diego, and I am sixteen years old. I moved back to Mexico with my parents and my little sister. Last week, I had four days off school. It was a special weekend because we celebrated the Day of the Dead.

On the first day, I got up late and ate eggs for breakfast. Then I went to the market with my mother. We bought orange flowers, fruit, and sweet bread called pan de muerto. At home, I helped clean the living room. My sister made colorful paper decorations.

On the second day, we prepared our family altar. We put a photo of my grandfather next to his favorite food. My grandmother told us stories about him. He loved music and often played the guitar. That evening, we ate together and talked about happy family memories.

On the third day, we visited the cemetery for the Day of the Dead. We took flowers and cleaned my grandfather's grave. I felt quiet, but I enjoyed listening to my grandmother. Later, I met my cousins. We walked around the town square and saw beautiful decorations. I took photographs with my phone.

On the fourth day, I stayed home. I did my homework, washed my shoes, and played a video game. Before dinner, I packed my school bag. I went to bed early because I felt tired. The weekend gave me time to rest and remember my grandfather.
